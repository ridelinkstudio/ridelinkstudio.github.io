About RideLink

Useful Digital Tools for Everyday Business

We help independent professionals and small businesses bring key information together in a simple, shareable digital format.

Many drivers and small businesses rely on referrals, phone calls, messaging apps and social media to reach customers. These channels are valuable, but business information can become scattered across posts and conversations.

RideLink offers practical digital solutions that help owners present their services, share business details and manage parts of their customer communication. Depending on the service selected, this can include a dedicated business page, a digital receipt booklet or promotional materials.

RideLink provides digital presentation and business-support tools. These services can help you communicate more clearly, but they do not guarantee enquiries, bookings, sales or a particular business outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RideLink only for drivers?

No. RideLink also offers digital business pages and receipt tools for entrepreneurs, retailers and other small businesses.

Do I need coding or technical skills?

No coding knowledge is required from customers ordering a page setup. RideLink handles the agreed page creation and setup.

Can customers contact me directly?

Where a direct contact option is included, visitors can use it to reach the business through the selected channel, such as WhatsApp.

How much do RideLink services cost?

Pricing depends on the service and package. Visit the relevant service page or contact us to confirm current prices and deliverables.

What is the RideLink Ambassador Programme?

It is a product-based referral opportunity. Ambassadors promote eligible RideLink and XTC Studio services and may earn 40% commission on eligible verified sales under the programme terms. It is not salaried employment and earnings are not guaranteed.
