About RideLink

Helping Businesses Be Easier to Find and Contact

Many independent drivers and small businesses rely on personal recommendations, phone calls, messaging apps and social media to reach customers. These channels are useful, but business information can become scattered across different conversations, posts and contact lists.

RideLink provides a simple way to bring important business information together on a dedicated online page. Depending on the service selected, a page can introduce a business, describe its services, display photographs and provide a direct contact option.

A shareable web address allows the owner to distribute that page through WhatsApp, social media, business cards, flyers and other appropriate channels. A customer can then open the page to learn more about the business before making an enquiry.

Our approach is practical: give independent professionals and smaller enterprises an accessible way to present their work without requiring them to build and manage an entire website from scratch.

RideLink is a digital business-presentation service. A professional page can help communicate what you offer, but it does not guarantee enquiries, sales, bookings or a particular business outcome.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some basic points to understand before deciding whether a RideLink page is suitable for you.

What exactly is RideLink?

RideLink is a digital business-presentation service that helps drivers, entrepreneurs and small businesses create dedicated online pages containing information about their services and ways to make contact.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding knowledge is required from the customer when ordering a page-creation service. RideLink handles the page setup according to the agreed requirements.

How can I share my page?

You can share your page address through messaging applications, social media, email and other appropriate communication channels. A QR code may also be available as part of your selected setup.

Will a RideLink page guarantee more customers?

No. A page provides a place to present your business information, but enquiries depend on many factors, including your services, pricing, reputation, audience and promotional activities.

How much does a RideLink page cost?

Pricing depends on the service and package selected. Contact RideLink to confirm the current price, included features and any additional costs before placing an order.

Can I request changes to my page?

Discuss your required changes with RideLink before ordering. The available customization and revision arrangements depend on the package agreed upon.

How do I contact RideLink?

You can use the contact link below to start a WhatsApp conversation and ask about available services, pricing and setup requirements.
